# Content Guide

## Tone

Friendly.

Professional.

Confident.

Never exaggerated.

Never overly promotional.

---

Writing Style

Short paragraphs.

Simple English.

Clear language.

No buzzwords.

No unnecessary adjectives.

---

Homepage

One clear sentence explaining who Lumi is.

One supporting paragraph.

One CTA.

---

About

Tell Lumi's story.

Focus on growth.

Focus on design + development + AI.

Do not list every skill.

---

Works

Each project should answer:

What?

Why?

Process?

Result?

---

Gallery

Minimal descriptions.

Let the artwork speak.

---

Contact

Simple.

Invite collaboration.

Examples:

Let's build something together.

Let's create thoughtful digital experiences.

I'd love to hear about your project.
